Why immutable

Every node boots the same image. Drift has nowhere to live.

Updates replace the whole OS atomically, and a failed upgrade boots back into the previous one.

What Kairos gives you

Bring any LinuxUbuntu, Fedora, Alpine, openSUSE and more, turned into an immutable system.
A/B upgradesThe whole OS updates as one image, with automatic rollback.
Kubernetes optionalShip k3s or k0s in the image, or run without a cluster.
