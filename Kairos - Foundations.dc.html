02

Color

Brand colors come straight from the logos. Light mode sits on warm stone; dark mode on violet ink, a low-chroma blue-violet that complements the orange. Never navy, never brown. Orange takes dark ink text; Hadron blue takes white.

Tiles, spacing and shape

The Kairos mark is built from rhombi in three tones. Tessellate lays them flat: a hexagon split into three rhombi, each in its logo tone. Fields fade in density, so a few tiles always fall away.
