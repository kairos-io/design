# Brand

Two marks, one system. **Kairos** is the project, the suite and the `kairos` CLI: the mark is eighteen rhombi in three oranges, a ring of six cubes. **Hadron** is a separate Linux distribution that ships with every Kairos release: it keeps its own name and its own logo, the same cube structure in three blues. The Foundations deck puts them side by side in its hero, "Kairos · Hadron", and states the rule for everything that follows: the marks do not change. Pick the rendition by the surface, never recolour, and never place a mark on an orange or blue fill or over a tile field.

The logos ship in this package unchanged, as SVG files under `assets/logos/` and as the `KairosMark`, `KairosLogo`, `HadronMark` and `HadronLogo` components. The Kairos artwork is the [CNCF artwork](https://github.com/cncf/artwork/tree/main/projects/kairos); the dark-surface lockups and the Hadron files are the decks' in this repository.

## Kairos

Three layouts, and the rendition by surface: colour on any plain light surface; on a dark surface the colour mark with the wordmark in white; white on a flat fill or photography; black only for one-colour print.

## Hadron

The mark is the same on light and dark surfaces, because the blues read on both. The lockup sets its wordmark in ink on light surfaces and in white on dark ones. There is a white mark for flat fills and photography and no one-colour lockup: on a fill, use the mark alone.

## Clear space and size

<ClearSpace />

The same rhombi, laid flat as a hexagon and tiled into fields, are the system's one decorative pattern, in both palettes. [Tiles](?path=/docs/foundations-tiles--docs) sets out the unit, the fields, the tile bar and the marker, and where each may go. A mark never sits on a tile field.

## Lockups in the interface

A product has its own name, repo and binary, and in UI chrome it shows the mark plus its bold name. Functionality is part of Kairos and has no product name. From the Naming deck:

- A product: the mark, "Kairos" in the regular weight, the product name in bold (Kairos **Installer**, Kairos **Factory**, Kairos **Operator**, Kairos **Lab**).
- The console: the mark and a bold "Kairos" alone. Its sections (Factory, Deploy, Fleet) are page titles, not names in the chrome.
- Hadron: its own mark and its own bold name. Never "Kairos Hadron".
- The favicon is the mark alone, colour rendition. The marks are for favicons, product lockups and app chrome.
- The two together, as on the Foundations hero and on hadron-linux.io ("Hadron v0.5.1 · by Kairos"): both lockups at the same height, Kairos first, a middle dot between them.
