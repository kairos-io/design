# Radius, elevation and motion

## Radius

One radius, 4px, and two neighbours: 2px for tags and chips, 8px for dialogs. The Tailwind `rounded-sm`, `rounded-base` and `rounded-lg` utilities map onto them through `tailwind.css`; every other `rounded-*` step is removed so a component cannot reach for a 10px corner. `rounded-full` stays for dots.

<RadiusScale />

## Elevation

No drop shadows anywhere. Elevation is a tone step: the canvas (`--bg`), a surface on it (`--surface`), and a raised element on the surface (`--raised`). Cards have no border; the step is the edge. Borders are 1px hairlines in `--line`, or 1.5px for inputs, the select and the outline button. The Tailwind `shadow-*` utilities are removed by `tailwind.css`, so a component cannot cast one.

<ElevationSteps />

## Motion

One easing and three durations. Hover changes use `fast`; anything that opens uses `base`; the one entrance animation, `fade-up`, uses `slow`, as does a tile landing. All of it is transform and opacity only, and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets every duration to zero and turns the animations off.
